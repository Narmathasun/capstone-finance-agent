---
title: Debt Payoff Strategies: Snowball vs Avalanche
source: Internal Financial Education Team
category: goal_planning
---
When paying off multiple debts, two popular strategies structure the order
of repayment differently.

The Debt Snowball method pays off the smallest balance first (while making
minimum payments on everything else), then rolls that payment into the
next-smallest balance, and so on. This method prioritizes psychological
momentum — quick "wins" from eliminating small debts can build motivation
to keep going.

The Debt Avalanche method instead pays off the highest-interest-rate debt
first, which minimizes total interest paid over time and is mathematically
optimal. 

Neither is universally "correct" — the avalanche method saves more money
in most cases, but the snowball method's early wins can improve follow-
through for people who struggle with long-term motivation. The best method
is the one a person will actually stick with consistently.
