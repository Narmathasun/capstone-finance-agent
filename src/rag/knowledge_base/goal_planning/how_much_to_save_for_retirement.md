---
title: How Much to Save for Retirement
source: Internal Financial Education Team
category: goal_planning
---
There's no single right answer, but several rules of thumb help create a
starting target. A common guideline suggests saving 15% of pre-tax income
annually for retirement (including any employer match), starting in your
20s or 30s.

Salary-multiple benchmarks are another approach: aiming to have
approximately 1x your salary saved by age 30, 3x by 40, 6x by 50, 8x by 60,
and 10x by retirement age (around 67), assuming a moderate savings rate and
market returns.

These are rough guides, not guarantees — actual needs depend heavily on
desired retirement lifestyle, expected Social Security benefits, healthcare
costs, and how long retirement will last. The earlier you start, the more
compound growth does the heavy lifting; someone starting at 25 needs to
save a meaningfully smaller percentage of income than someone starting the
same target at 45.
